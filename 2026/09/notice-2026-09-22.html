Approve First Amendment to Amended and Restated Renewable Power Purchase Agreement with Noble Solar LLC to Increase Community Benefits, Guaranteed Efficiency Rate and Curtailment Threshold; Reduce Cost of Excess Deliveries, and Allow Further Extension to Development Cure Period in case of Continued Delay in Issuance of Federal Permits
Renewable procurement & PPAs · proposed · priority 4/5 · packet p.3 · packet
The Board is proposed to approve amendments to the Purple Sage Energy Center PPA (400 MW solar + 1,600 MWh storage in Nevada) to extend development cure periods due to federal permit delays, increase community benefits from $1M to $1.5M, improve efficiency rates, and reduce excess delivery costs.
Why it matters: Amendment to a major long-term renewable PPA is critical to Community Power's 100% clean energy target and Resource Adequacy compliance strategy.
Priority rationale: Modifies a significant long-term renewable contract that underpins procurement strategy and RPS compliance; delay extensions and increased community benefits have material policy implications.
Adopt Resolution No. 2026-14, Approving a Fiscal and Financial Training Policy Pursuant to Senate Bill 827 (SB 827)
Board governance changes · proposed · priority 2/5 · packet p.3 · packet
The Board is proposed to adopt a policy designating certain staff positions required to complete biennial fiscal and financial training as mandated by SB 827, effective January 1, 2026.
Why it matters: SB 827 compliance is a regulatory requirement affecting governance and fiduciary oversight practices.
Priority rationale: Mandatory regulatory compliance for fiscal governance training; procedural but not substantive to alliance policy interests.
What changed from the previous version
The First Amendment to the Noble Solar LLC PPA has been moved from the consent calendar to a formal action item on the regular agenda (Item 11, page 119), and additional details have been provided in an expanded staff report. The amendment now includes: an extended Development Cure Period of up to 180 additional days for Guaranteed Construction Start and 120 additional days for Guaranteed Commercial Operation (if federal permit delays continue); increased Community Benefits from $1.0M to $1.5M (shown in Item 11 Attachment A Section 2(j) as "$1,500,000"); improved Guaranteed Efficiency Rate (referenced in Exhibit A); increased Curtailment Threshold; and reduced Excess Deliveries costs. The item is now proposed for Board action rather than being on the consent calendar as previously analyzed. No other material changes to the three informational items (SBS Program, EDAM Update) or the governance items (Coronado accession, SB 827 training policy).
