Update on Dynamic Pricing Pilot (HFP) and Vehicle-Grid Integration (VGI) Pilot
Equity & low-income programs · information only · priority 3/5 · packet p.17 · packet
Staff updates board on two CPUC-authorized 2-year pilots (ending 12/31/27): Hourly Flex Pricing (HFP) expanded agricultural pilot with 20 participants (~1.2MW) and Vehicle-Grid Integration pilot with 5 participants (~5MW); midterm evaluation shows mixed results including low customer price engagement and design issues, with PG&E proposing HFP 2.0 for 2029.
Why it matters: Dynamic pricing and demand response pilots test customer engagement models and may inform rate design; VGI represents emerging vehicle-grid technology deployment.
Priority rationale: Experimental pilots exploring customer demand flexibility and EV integration; useful for rate strategy but not immediate policy action required.
