Proposed Third Amended and Restated Schedule A.1 to Master Services Agreement with CLEAResult Consulting, Inc
Local renewable development · proposed · priority 3/5 · packet p.2 · packet
The board will consider approving an amendment to extend CLEAResult's Energy Management Program contract through March 31, 2027, with an increase to the cumulative not-to-exceed compensation from $3,268,141 to $6,500,000 to meet increased demand for the program's energy efficiency services (including fuel-switching projects like heat pump water heaters).
Why it matters: This energy efficiency program serves MCE's non-residential customers and achieves both energy savings (reduced over 5 GWh annually to date) and clean energy outcomes, directly supporting MCE's clean energy mission and local energy efficiency development.
Priority rationale: Worth tracking as a significant expansion of a CPUC-funded energy efficiency program, though primarily operational rather than strategic policy.
