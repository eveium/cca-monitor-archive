Approval of EEI Master Agreement and Collateral Annex with J.P. Morgan Ventures Energy Corp.
Renewable procurement & PPAs · proposed · priority 3/5 · packet p.2 · packet
MCE staff recommends approval to enter into an Edison Electric Institute (EEI) Master Power Purchase and Sale Agreement and associated Collateral Annex with J.P. Morgan Ventures Energy Corporation to enable MCE to transact for a variety of energy products, including CAISO energy hedges. The Master Agreement has no independent contract value and does not represent a commitment to any specific transaction; future transactions under subsequent Confirmations would be subject to applicable approval authorities and processes.
Why it matters: Master agreements for energy transactions are foundational to CCA renewable procurement and hedging strategies, enabling the organization to efficiently manage energy procurement and manage price volatility.
Priority rationale: Routine approval of a standard master agreement framework that enables future energy transactions; significant only if it materially changes MCE's hedging or procurement flexibility.
MCE 2025 Power Source Disclosure Attestation
Renewable procurement & PPAs · proposed · priority 2/5 · packet p.2 · packet
The board will consider approving MCE's 2025 power source disclosure attestation, which documents the energy sources (renewable, nuclear, fossil) in MCE's generation mix.
Why it matters: Power source disclosure is critical to demonstrating MCE's compliance with renewable energy procurement targets and progress toward clean energy goals.
Priority rationale: Annual compliance reporting on energy mix; worth tracking to monitor progress on renewable procurement targets but routine unless MCE's renewable percentage has declined significantly.

San Diego Community Power Data Center Load Integration Guidelines
Local renewable development · proposed · priority 3/5 · packet p.4 · packet
The board will consider approving Data Center Load Integration Guidelines, a working framework to guide Community Power's engagement with data center electrical loads (20+ MW non-interruptible loads) requiring dedicated long-term capacity planning.
Why it matters: Addresses local development of renewable resources and capacity planning to serve large loads, with potential implications for procurement strategy and grid integration.
Priority rationale: Establishes operational framework for a growing customer segment with significant resource planning implications; CAC unanimously recommended approval.
2025 Power Source Disclosure & Power Content Label (PCL); and Adoption of Resolution No. 2026-13, Approving the Submission and Attesting to the Accuracy of Community Power's 2025 Power Source Disclosure Annual Reports for PowerBase, PowerOn, Power100, and Power100 Green+
Renewable procurement & PPAs · proposed · priority 3/5 · packet p.4 · packet
The board will consider adopting Resolution 2026-13 to approve and attest to the accuracy of Community Power's 2025 Power Source Disclosure annual reports for all service offerings, which is required annual regulatory filing addressing the energy mix and carbon intensity of the utility's power supply.
Why it matters: Power Source Disclosure and Power Content Labels are key regulatory compliance documents that detail Community Power's renewable energy procurement performance and RPS compliance.
Priority rationale: Routine annual regulatory filing required by CPUC to demonstrate RPS compliance and renewable energy procurement portfolio mix.
