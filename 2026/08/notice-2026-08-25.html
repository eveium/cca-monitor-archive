San Diego Community Power Data Center Load Integration Guidelines
Local renewable development · proposed · priority 3/5 · packet p.4 · packet
The board will consider approving Data Center Load Integration Guidelines, a working framework to guide Community Power's engagement with data center electrical loads (20+ MW non-interruptible loads) requiring dedicated long-term capacity planning.
Why it matters: Addresses local development of renewable resources and capacity planning to serve large loads, with potential implications for procurement strategy and grid integration.
