Disadvantaged Communities (DAC) PPA Amendments
Equity & low-income programs · proposed · priority 4/5 · packet
The board will consider approving amendments to two Power Purchase Agreements with solar project developers to extend the allowable delay days from 180 to 365 before triggering an Event of Default, accommodating interconnection delays from PG&E. Both projects (San Leandro 1 MW and Hayward 0.56 MW) are expected to achieve commercial operation in spring 2027 and will serve approximately 3,920 DAC program customers.
Why it matters: This directly addresses Ava's DAC-GT program commitments to serve low-income customers in disadvantaged communities with 100% renewable energy at a 20% bill discount.
Priority rationale: Material amendments to DAC renewable projects serving low-income customers; delays in interconnection are operationally significant but projects remain on track for 2027.
Ava & Lunar Agreement: Amendment 2 and 3
Local renewable development · proposed · priority 3/5 · packet
The board will consider approving two amendments to the Lunar Energy contract to refine the SmartHome Charging program scope, extend the managed charging end date from March 31, 2027 to December 31, 2027, and allow Lunar to subcontract with automakers as additional managed charging providers.
Why it matters: The SmartHome Charging program is a key VPP initiative supporting local EV load management, demand flexibility, and emissions reduction across Ava's service territory.
Priority rationale: Program expansion and operational refinement based on Year 1 learnings; moderate importance to Ava's local DER and VPP strategy.
Amendment to Rate Setting Policy
Rate setting & PCIA · proposed · priority 2/5 · packet
The board will consider adopting amendments to the Rate Setting Policy to update the agency name from East Bay Community Energy to Ava Community Energy, streamline committee language to allow staff greater administrative flexibility, and establish formal accessibility standards for the rate-setting process.
Why it matters: Updates to rate-setting procedures affect how Ava proposes and implements rate changes, which influence customer rates and PCIA competitiveness.
Priority rationale: Primarily administrative housekeeping and process streamlining; no material change to rate philosophy or competitiveness strategy.
Revised NEM Tariff
Rate setting & PCIA · proposed · priority 2/5 · packet
The board will consider approving revisions to the Net Energy Metering (NEM) Policy Tariff to update terminology (replacing 'true-up' with 'billing plan') and clarify the treatment of NEM Fuel Cell customers, who are ineligible for net surplus electricity compensation under state law.
Why it matters: NEM tariff changes affect customer billing practices, net metering credits, and cash-out procedures for renewable customers, impacting rate design and equity.
Priority rationale: Clarification of existing policy and terminology alignment; no substantive policy change to NEM treatment or rates.
2026 Legislative Roundup and 2027 Look Ahead
Legislative positions · information only · priority 3/5 · packet
Staff will provide an informational report on Ava's 2026 legislative activity and outlook for 2027, highlighting AB 1761 (a CalCCA-sponsored bill on PCIA transparency) which has passed the legislature and is awaiting the Governor's signature.
Why it matters: Tracks Ava's legislative advocacy and policy positions, including landmark PCIA transparency bill that advances CCA interests and customer awareness.
Priority rationale: AB 1761 is a significant CalCCA-led legislative achievement on PCIA transparency; merits tracking of future legislative priorities.
