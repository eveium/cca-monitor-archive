Recommend Board Adopt Resolution Amending the Community Clean Energy Grant Program Policy
Equity & low-income programs · proposed · priority 3/5 · packet p.131 · packet
Recommends Board adoption of resolution to amend the Community Clean Energy Grant Program Policy to include framework for administering Community Benefits funds from PPAs and rename policy to 'Community Clean Energy Benefits and Grants Policy.' Community Power anticipates $2.8 million in community benefit funds over next three years from nine PPAs.
Why it matters: Expands Community Power's equity and community benefit programming by formalizing administration of developer-funded community benefits tied to renewable energy PPAs.
Priority rationale: Significant community investment mechanism ($2.8M over 3 years) for workforce training, energy infrastructure, and environmental benefits in low-income communities; builds on successful grant program track record.
Update on Regional Energy Markets: The Extended Day-Ahead Market
State regulatory proceedings · information only · priority 3/5 · packet p.151 · packet
Informational update on CAISO's Extended Day-Ahead Market (EDAM) launched May 1, 2026, which expands regional coordination of utility-scale energy resources into day-ahead timeframe. Community Power is a market participant in both WEIM and EDAM. Discusses benefits for affordability, reliability, and sustainability through broader resource coordination.
Why it matters: EDAM represents significant market development affecting Community Power's procurement costs, renewable curtailment reduction, and resource scheduling efficiency.
Priority rationale: EDAM launch and governance transition (via AB 825) create new market opportunities and operational efficiencies for Community Power's energy procurement and customer cost management.
Receive and File Information Related to the Addition of the City of Coronado as a Member of San Diego Community Power
Board governance changes · information only · priority 4/5 · packet p.155 · packet
Staff presents feasibility study and analysis regarding the addition of the City of Coronado as a new member agency of Community Power. Coronado City Council unanimously approved ordinance/resolution on July 21, 2026 to seek membership. Pro forma analysis estimates net revenues of approximately $1.2 million over 2028-2029 and $1.8 million in 2030.
Why it matters: Expansion of Community Power's member agencies affects governance structure, voting shares (increases from 7 to 8 members), and operational scale.
Priority rationale: New member agency addition changes governance structure, voting allocation, and organizational scope; first new member since National City (2021).

Proposed Fiscal Year End 2027 Budget Amendment with Increased Temporary Rate Credit
Rate setting & PCIA · proposed · priority 5/5 · packet p.1 · packet
The Executive Committee will consider recommending to the Board an amendment to increase the temporary rate credit from $0.0062/kWh to up to $0.03725/kWh effective November 1, 2026 through March 31, 2027, funded by increasing the Operating Reserve Fund withdrawal from $62.0 million to $85.9 million. Why it matters: This directly addresses rate competitiveness with IOUs and the Power Charge Indifference Adjustment (PCIA) impact on customer bills, core issues affecting MCE's rate setting and competitive positioning.
Priority rationale: Major rate relief action that affects all customer classes and reserve strategy; sets precedent for FYE 2028 budget and PCIA mitigation approach.
