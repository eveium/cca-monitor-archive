California Community Choice Association's Comments on the Proposed Decision Resolving Track Two Issues (R.25-02-005)
Rate setting & PCIA · information only · priority 5/5 · packet
CalCCA files extensive comments opposing the Proposed Decision on pre-2019 banked renewable energy credits (RECs), arguing that the decision violates section 366.2(g)'s requirement to account for benefits that remain with bundled customers. CalCCA contends that customers who paid for banked RECs while bundled but later departed for CCA service should receive their share of compliance benefits when those RECs are used by IOUs.
Why it matters: This is a major PCIA litigation involving the allocation of renewable energy benefits between bundled and unbundled customers and the potential cost shifts between them under California's power charge indifference framework.
Priority rationale: This Track 2 decision concerns the statutory obligation to prevent cost shifts between bundled and unbundled customers on an issue that has been litigated for six years; addresses a fundamental interpretation of section 366.2(g) indifference requirements.

Priority rationale: Relevant to equity and low-income affordability programs, though the joint proposal represents consensus between CCA and IOU rather than contentious advocacy.
