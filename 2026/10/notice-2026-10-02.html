Proposed Fiscal Year End 2027 Budget Amendment with Increased Temporary Rate Credit
Rate setting & PCIA · proposed · priority 5/5 · packet p.1 · packet
The Executive Committee will consider recommending to the Board an amendment to increase the temporary rate credit from $0.0062/kWh to up to $0.03725/kWh effective November 1, 2026 through March 31, 2027, funded by increasing the Operating Reserve Fund withdrawal from $62.0 million to $85.9 million. The proposal aims to bring MCE's generation charges in line with or below PG&E's rates, including PCIA impacts.
Why it matters: This directly addresses rate competitiveness with IOUs and the Power Charge Indifference Adjustment (PCIA) impact on customer bills, core issues affecting MCE's rate setting and competitive positioning.
Priority rationale: Major rate relief action that affects all customer classes and reserve strategy; sets precedent for FYE 2028 budget and PCIA mitigation approach.
